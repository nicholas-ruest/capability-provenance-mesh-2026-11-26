# ADR-016: Autogenous repair and rollback

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: Autogenous proposes only in quarantine and a new digest reruns all evidence before approval.

## Rejected alternative
in-place edits erase provenance; generated patches have no authority. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `crates/cpm-autogenous/src/lib.rs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `crates/cpm-autogenous/tests/rollback.rs`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
recovery links denied, proposed, evaluated, and promoted digests. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
