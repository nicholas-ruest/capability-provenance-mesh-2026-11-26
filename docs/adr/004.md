# ADR-004: Effective authority is a monotone intersection

**Status:** Accepted

## Security invariant
A candidate must never gain authority while moving through observation and policy.

## Rule adopted
Intersect declared, requested, observed, and allowed resource/action/scope triples. Unknown or widening claims deny the case.

## Implementation surface
- Domain or production source: [crates/cpm-capability-model/src/lib.rs](../../crates/cpm-capability-model/src/lib.rs)
- Co-located test or reciprocal caller: [crates/cpm-application/src/bin/ablation.rs](../../crates/cpm-application/src/bin/ablation.rs)

## Why the simpler route fails
Risk scores cannot prove set containment and benign signals can dilute one forbidden action.

## Observable acceptance
The attenuation-off experiment must admit an unsafe fixture blocked by full composition.
