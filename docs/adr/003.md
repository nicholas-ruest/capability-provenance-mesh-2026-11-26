# ADR-003: Stable domain contracts

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: cpm-contracts normalizes identity, findings, grants, observations, and outcomes across seven tools.

## Rejected alternative
arbitrary JSON hides drift; upstream models must not own product semantics. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `crates/cpm-contracts/src/lib.rs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `crates/cpm-contracts/tests/schema_roundtrip.rs`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
Rust round-trip and OpenAPI drift bind one version. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
