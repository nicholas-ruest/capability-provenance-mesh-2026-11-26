# ADR-001: Production-reachable modular monolith

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: one Rust process with cpm-service constructing cpm-application and typed domain, store, and adapter ports.

## Rejected alternative
distributed services add consistency failure; a single crate hides ownership. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `crates/cpm-service/src/composition.rs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `crates/cpm-service/tests/composition_graph.rs`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
locked metadata and composition schema 2 prove every crate is reachable. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
