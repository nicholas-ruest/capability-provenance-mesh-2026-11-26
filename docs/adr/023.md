# ADR-023: Rootless compatible topology

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: separate API/web images share one volume and compatible host or none networking.

## Rejected alternative
privileged containers change policy; smoke-only launch is incomplete. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `containers/Containerfile.api`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `tests/containers/readiness.sh`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
suite builds, launches, saves, retrieves, and stops. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
