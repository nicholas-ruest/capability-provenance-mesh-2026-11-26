# DDD-008: Receipt and audit vocabulary

## Purpose and scope
ExecutionReceipt proves an operation; DecisionRecord states governance; AuditEntry orders domain facts. They share correlation but are not interchangeable.

## State constraints
Raw output hashes remain in receipts. Store append order is durable; projections may be rebuilt without rewriting facts.

## Realization
| Concern | Existing path |
|---|---|
| aggregate behavior | [crates/cpm-receipts/src/lib.rs](../../crates/cpm-receipts/src/lib.rs) |
| integration or failure proof | [crates/cpm-store/src/lib.rs](../../crates/cpm-store/src/lib.rs) |

## Completion criterion
Receipt validation and restart readback establish causal continuity.
