# DDD-004: Evidence case aggregate

## Ubiquitous language
An EvidenceCase joins lineage, normalized observations, trial terminal state, graph completeness, and canonical receipts for one digest.

## Aggregate rule
Evidence from another digest and dangling graph edges cannot satisfy the case. Missing required evidence yields Incomplete.

The implemented owner is [crates/cpm-effect-graph/src/lib.rs](../../crates/cpm-effect-graph/src/lib.rs). The reciprocal caller/test is [crates/cpm-observation/src/lib.rs](../../crates/cpm-observation/src/lib.rs).

## Evidence of the boundary
Graph failure tests and the graph-off experiment expose the boundary.
