# ADR-007: SkillSpector as evidence

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: pinned SkillSpector findings are normalized while execution or parse errors become IncompleteEvidence.

## Rejected alternative
zero findings on failure is unsafe; adapters cannot own policy. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `crates/cpm-skillspector/src/lib.rs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `crates/cpm-scan/tests/incomplete_evidence.rs`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
benign, malicious, and failure logs are source-bound. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
