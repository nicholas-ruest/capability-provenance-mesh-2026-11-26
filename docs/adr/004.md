# ADR-004: Quarantine before inspection

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: candidate bytes are digested and copied into immutable quarantine before parsing or execution.

## Rejected alternative
in-place inspection creates effects; filenames are not identities. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `crates/cpm-intake/src/quarantine.rs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `crates/cpm-quarantine/tests/parent_unchanged.rs`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
denial proves unchanged parent and dispatch hashes. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
