# ADR-011: Incomplete evidence as refusal

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: timeouts, parse errors, and missing provenance become typed IncompleteEvidence and never promote.

## Rejected alternative
collapsing faults into denial obscures retryability. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `crates/cpm-policy/src/outcome.rs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `crates/cpm-application/tests/no_registry_write.rs`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
incomplete makes zero registry or dispatch writes. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
