# ADR-024: Exact-clean Rust source floor

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: pinned helper scans all recognized tracked source with hygiene and fifty percent genuine Rust.

## Rejected alternative
badges, extensions, padding, and hidden languages are forbidden. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `scripts/dream-machine-rust-share.mjs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `scripts/tests/rust-share-negative.sh`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
external inventory and exact-head step bind one SHA. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
