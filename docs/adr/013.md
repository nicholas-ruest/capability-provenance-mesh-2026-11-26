# ADR-013: Remediation creates a new proposal and digest

**Status:** Accepted

## Trigger
Repair must remain attributable and cannot rewrite the bytes or decision that were denied.

## Outcome
Bind changes and explanation to the denied parent, then re-enter intake as a new candidate. Generated content has no promotion authority.

The controlling implementation is [crates/cpm-remediation/src/lib.rs](../../crates/cpm-remediation/src/lib.rs); its reciprocal production/test boundary is [crates/cpm-upstreams/src/autogenous.rs](../../crates/cpm-upstreams/src/autogenous.rs).

## Option declined
In-place patching severs evidence; auto-activation bypasses human review.

## Evidence contract
Recovery trace shows parent, new digest, repeated evaluation, and no activation of the parent.
