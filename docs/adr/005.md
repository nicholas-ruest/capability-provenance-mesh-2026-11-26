# ADR-005: Monotonic authority attenuation

**Status:** Accepted for implementation

## Context and decision
Capability Provenance Mesh must produce a complete source-bound result while preserving human authority and no-effect denial. The decision is: resource/action/scope triples intersect declared, requested, observed, and allowed authority and never widen.

## Rejected alternative
string tags hide aliases; scan output cannot grant authority. This alternative fails a product invariant or makes exact-source evidence ambiguous.

## Consequences and ownership
The production owner is `crates/cpm-capabilities/src/algebra.rs`. It exposes a typed boundary, preserves raw upstream receipts, and fails closed. The behavior or failure test is `crates/cpm-capabilities/tests/monotonicity.rs`. No adapter may call the accepted registry directly; cpm-application remains the transaction boundary.

## Acceptance evidence
ablation exposes the forbidden fixture blocked by attenuation. Evidence comes from real execution outside the checkout and binds the exact clean source SHA. A change requires a superseding ADR and reciprocal updates to crate, test, DDD record, and composition report.
